How accounting runs in the ERP

One guide for people using the Accounting screens day to day and for anyone demonstrating them to a customer. It follows the books from first setup to year-end close and shows which screen does what, who uses it, and which entries the system posts on its own.

Eight stages, repeated every month

Setup happens once. Stages 2 to 8 repeat each month. Pick a stage to see its screens, the steps, and what happens in the books.

Most entries post themselves

Your team works in Sales, Purchase, Inventory and HRMS as usual. When a document is approved or posted there, Accounting writes the journal automatically. Accountants key in only what no other module covers, using vouchers and journals.

If an automatic entry cannot post, the document in Sales or Purchase still saves. The entry waits in Accounting › Posting Failures. Fix the cause (usually a missing account in the Chart of Accounts) and press Retry.

Which voucher to use

Vouchers are ready-made journal forms for common transactions. Pick the one that matches what happened and the system fills in the right side for you. Use a plain Journal only when no voucher fits.

SituationUse
Paying rent, electricity or a vendor outside the Purchase modulePayment Voucher
Money received that is not a sales receipt: interest, a refund, owner's capitalReceipt Voucher
Cash deposited in bank, cash withdrawn, transfer between two own bank accountsContra Voucher
Giving a customer a discount or price correction after the invoiceCredit Note
Asking a vendor for a price correction after their billDebit Note
Accruals, provisions, reclassifications, opening balances, correctionsJournal
A wrong entry that is already postedOpen it and press Reverse. Posted entries are never edited or deleted, so the audit trail stays complete.

A 20-minute customer demo

Tell one story from start to finish: a trading company buys goods, sells them, gets paid, and closes the month. Each step shows one screen, and the italic line is what to say. Prepare a demo company that already has a month of sample data, so every report has numbers in it.

If the customer asks “Can my accountant still pass manual entries?”, show Journals › New, enter a ₹2,00,000 provision, and show that it lands in Pending Approvals for a second person. That one answer covers control, audit and flexibility.
